    Obstacle planning

    Clearance is measured from the drone’s centre. 0.30 m suits this 51 cm drone; openings it keeps are flown straight through.

    Avoidance zones & holes

      Most precise: hold the drone in the middle of the hole and press “Hole at drone”. Hole control (avoid mode, hole inside a zone you drew): the drone hovers 1.5 m before the hole until its measured centre stops moving, lines up, then flies straight through.
